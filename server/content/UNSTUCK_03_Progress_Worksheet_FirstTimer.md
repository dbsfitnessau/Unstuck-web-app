## How to use this worksheet

Seven tests. Each one gets its own page. Day 0 on the left, Day 28 on the right — same page, no flipping back and forth. Fill in the pass/fail boxes first. Then the number. Take the photo. Move on.

That's it.

**The promise:** if you fill this out honestly, on Day 28 you'll be able to see exactly what's changed — and what hasn't.

**The honest bit:** these are field tests, not clinical assessments. Treat changes under ~1 cm or ~5° as noise. Treat anything beyond that as real.

---

## 🛑 Stop Signs (read once, remember for 28 days)

While training:

- **Sharp, stabbing pain** in any position → stop immediately. Drop the level by one tier or skip the exercise.
- **Radiating pain, pins-and-needles, or numbness** → stop and see a physio. Don't push through nerve symptoms.
- **Pain that gets worse rep-to-rep** → that's not "working through it." Back off the depth or the load.
- **Joint clicking with pain** → stop. (Painless clicking is usually fine.)
- **Symptoms lingering more than ~48–72 hours, or getting worse day-to-day** → reduce volume next session and reassess.

Mobility work should be uncomfortable at end range. It should not be painful in the medical sense. Learn the difference.

## Test 2 — Wall Ankle (Knee-to-Wall)

\[ILLUSTRATION — Side-on, foot perpendicular to wall, knee driving forward, heel firmly down. Measurement arrow from toe to wall.\]

\[QR CODE — 30-second video demo\]

**The Setup**
- Face the wall in a half-kneel.
- Front foot perpendicular to the wall, toe close to it.
- Drive your knee forward to touch the wall — **heel must stay glued down**.
- Inch the foot back until the knee *just* stops touching with the heel still down.
- Measure toe-to-wall in cm. Do both sides.

## Test 4 — Wall Shoulder Flexion

\[ILLUSTRATION — Side-on, full body against wall (back, head, bum, heels all touching), arms reaching overhead. Gap arrow from wrist to wall.\]

\[QR CODE — 30-second video demo\]

**The Setup**
- Stand with back, head, bum, **and heels** against the wall. (This is the UNSTUCK standard — match it on both tests.)
- Arms straight, palms facing each other, lift overhead.
- Try to touch the wall behind you without arching your lower back off it.
- If hands don't touch, measure wrist-to-wall in cm.

## Test 5 — Thomas Test (Self-Administered)

\[ILLUSTRATION — Side-on, lying back on a bench, hugging one knee to chest, other leg hanging off the edge. Horizontal dashed line at the bench surface. Knee bend angle marked at the hanging leg.\]

\[QR CODE — 30-second video demo\]

**The Setup**
- Sit at the very edge of a bench or bed.
- Lie back, hugging one knee firmly to your chest (this locks your pelvis).
- Let the other leg hang off the edge naturally — don't push it down.
- Look at where the thigh sits and how the knee bends.
- Swap legs. Phone propped at the foot of the bench, side-on.

**The Check** *(remember: knee bend is measured **at the knee joint** — calf hanging back toward the bench leg = ~80°+)*

| | Day 0 | Day 28 |
|:---|:---:|:---:|
| **Left leg:** thigh at or below horizontal? | ☐ Y ☐ N | ☐ Y ☐ N |
| **Left leg:** knee bend ~80°+? | ☐ Y ☐ N | ☐ Y ☐ N |
| **Right leg:** thigh at or below horizontal? | ☐ Y ☐ N | ☐ Y ☐ N |
| **Right leg:** knee bend ~80°+? | ☐ Y ☐ N | ☐ Y ☐ N |
| Tighter side | Left / Right / Even | Left / Right / Even |

## Test 6 — Seated Thoracic Rotation

\[ILLUSTRATION — Top-down view, seated with a cushion between the knees (to lock the hips), arms crossed, torso rotated. Clock-face overlay showing 12 / 1 / 2 / 3 reference.\]

\[QR CODE — 30-second video demo\]

**The Setup**
- Sit on a chair or kneel. Cushion or block between the knees (it locks your hips so the rotation is *thoracic*).
- Cross arms over chest.
- Rotate as far as you can to each side **without lifting your hips**.
- Estimate against a clock face: 1 o'clock ≈ 30°, 2 o'clock ≈ 60°. Healthy range is roughly 40–45°+ per side.

**The Check**

## Test 7 — Cossack Squat Depth

\[ILLUSTRATION — Front-on, wide stance, weight shifted to one side, hip below knee, opposite leg straight with foot flexed.\]

\[QR CODE — 30-second video demo\]

**The Setup**
- Wide stance, toes slightly turned out.
- Shift weight to one leg, sink down.
- Keep the loaded heel **down**. Hip drops below the knee. Straight leg stays straight with the foot flexed.
- Swap sides.

## A note on sources

The seven tests draw on widely used physiotherapy and movement-assessment protocols. Dig deeper if you want:

- **Wall Ankle / Knee-to-Wall Test:** Bennell et al. (1998), *Australian Journal of Physiotherapy*.
- **Thomas Test (~80° knee flexion criterion):** Magee, *Orthopedic Physical Assessment*.
- **Stretching and ROM adaptations:** Behm & Chaouachi (2011); Medeiros et al. (2016).
- **Thoracic rotation norms:** AAOS *Joint Motion: Method of Measuring and Recording*.

These are field-test adaptations, not clinical assessments. If anything feels off in your body during the programme, see a physio.
